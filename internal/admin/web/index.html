Devices

loading...
The agent (android/agent) is the device owner, set over adb by android/enroll.sh <serial> on the host of the device: no factory reset, no account (Google) on the device. It keeps the system launcher, grants no permission and installs nothing by itself, always forbids safe mode. adb alone cannot remove it: "Release the device" or android/release.sh. Every device has its configuration mcloud-<serial> in Headwind MDM.
